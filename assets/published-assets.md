## The SaaS Tax Series

| # | URL | Title | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part 1 | https://www.appunite.com/blog/business-paying-for-100-saas-using-12 | The SaaS Tax — You're Paying for 100%, Using 12% | **Series opener / top-of-funnel.** Establishes the problem: companies subsidize SaaS features built for someone else while missing what they actually need. Applies the framework to the ATS decision. Transferable methodology — any reader can use this for their own stack. Bridges the manifesto declaration and the discovery methodology. |
| Part 4 | https://www.appunite.com/blog/how-to-discover-whats-actually-broken-in-your-saas-tool | How to Discover What's Actually Broken in Your SaaS Tool | **Methodology article.** Step-by-step process for converting vague SaaS frustrations into quantified, documented problems. The process that produced the 24 problems and 150,648 PLN cost estimate. Replicable framework any team lead can run in a week. Precedes Part 5 in the series. |
| Part 5 | https://www.appunite.com/blog/what-we-found-wrong-with-our-ats | 24 Problems, 7 Clusters — What We Found Wrong with Our ATS | **Deep-dive / evidence article.** All 24 problems across all 7 clusters, with full cost breakdown. Top-3 deep dives (Cluster 1: reporting errors, Cluster 5: interview feedback, Cluster 4: competency matrices). Meta-pattern: structural limitations, not bugs. Transferable lens for readers' own tools. Sets up Part 6 (does the cost justify building?). |
| Part 6 | https://www.appunite.com/blog/how-to-estimate-the-roi-of-replacing-a-saas-tool | How to Estimate the ROI of Replacing a SaaS Tool (With Real Numbers) | **ROI methodology article.** Two-bucket framework (direct vs. opportunity costs). Appunite's real numbers: 150,648 PLN/yr identified costs, 86,000 PLN build budget, -74% ROI on direct costs alone, +75% including opportunity costs. 4-step methodology for running the calculation yourself. Budget ceiling formula. Honest uncertainty framing. Bridges to Part 7 (scoping decision). |
