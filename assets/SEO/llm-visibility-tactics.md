# Actionable LLM Optimization Tactics

## **Off-Page Signals (Highest Priority)**

**Core finding:** Branded web mentions showed the strongest correlation with AI overview appearances across all factors studied.

### High-Value Citation Sources
- **Reddit:** Most-cited domain (6% of all ChatGPT/Perplexity citations)
- **User-generated content:** Forums, Quora, Reddit threads
- **Third-party reviews:** G2, Capterra, CNET, Forbes
- **YouTube:** Disproportionately high citation rate relative to presence
- **Wikipedia:** Actually cited LESS often (models already trained on it)

### Action Steps
- Get brand mentioned in relevant Reddit threads (even non-linked mentions count)
- Secure listings on review platforms
- Create/optimize YouTube content with transcripts
- Build presence on UGC platforms in your category

---

## **Content Types That Perform**

### Citation rates by format:
- **Comparative listicles:** 1 in 3 AI citations
- **Blog content:** 1 in 10 citations  
- **Research/data:** High citation rate
- **Video:** High citation rate
- **Interactive tools:** LOW citation rate (half expected traffic)
- **Listing/navigation pages:** LOW citation rate

### Relative to traditional search:
- LLMs send 3x more traffic to core site pages (about, contact, product)
- Documents/PDFs get more LLM traffic vs. zero traditional search traffic
- Research content performs significantly better in LLMs

---

## **On-Page Structure**

### Required elements:
1. **BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front)** - answer first, context second
2. **Declarative sentences** - confident, no hedging
3. **Simple sentence structure** - minimize subject-predicate hops
4. **Entity richness** - mention related products/topics/concepts explicitly
5. **Global document context** - periodically restate what document covers
6. **TL;DR sections** - at top of posts
7. **Q&A formatting** - FAQs written in natural language
8. **Tables over images** - LLMs can't process images well

### Avoid:
- Vague/wishy-washy language
- Complex nested sentence structures
- Image-based information
- Generic verbs without specifics

---

## **Content Freshness**

**Data:** LLMs prefer significantly newer content than traditional search results cite.

**Actions:**
- Update high-value pages regularly
- Add recent stats (1-2 years maximum age)
- Include current year in content where relevant
- Refresh pillar content quarterly

---

## **Technical Optimization**

### Critical issues:
- **JavaScript:** Many AI crawlers don't render JS - keep essential content in HTML
- **Hallucinated URLs:** 3.6% of Ahrefs' LLM traffic went to non-existent pages
- **Novel training sources:** GitHub, research papers, patents now matter

### Action steps:
1. Audit LLM referral traffic for 404s
2. Redirect commonly-hallucinated URLs
3. Ensure pillar content doesn't require JS rendering
4. Optimize GitHub repos, research citations

---

## **Strategic Content Architecture**

### Pillar strategy:
- **5-7 pillar pages** covering core topics
- **2,000+ words** per pillar
- **5-10 supporting posts** per pillar  
- **Internal linking** from spokes to hubs
- **Educational focus** not sales-heavy

### Prioritize creating:
- Comparison content ("X vs Y")
- Original research/data
- FAQ-style resources
- Specific use-case guides

---

## **Query Fanout Phenomenon**

**Finding:** Only 12% of LLM-cited links also rank in Google's top 10 for the same query.

**Why:** LLMs don't search the exact question asked. They generate simpler "fanout queries" - shorter, more product-focused sub-topics.

**Example:**  
- User asks: "best cheap sunglasses 2025"
- LLM searches: "men affordable sunglasses review 2025" + "best cheap men's sunglasses 2025"

**Action:** Create content targeting likely fanout variations of your core topics.

---

## **Entity Gap Analysis**

**Method:**
1. Identify prompts where competitors appear but you don't
2. Analyze context they're mentioned in
3. Create on-site + off-site content bridging that gap

**Example:** If competitor mentioned for "school backpacks" but you're not, create:
- Landing page about school-appropriate products  
- Outreach to affiliates for "back to school" content featuring your brand

---

## **Measurement Benchmarks**

### Citation drift:
- **47% of domains cited** changed within single month
- Requires frequent monitoring (weekly recommended)

### Traffic patterns:
- AI traffic growing rapidly (small absolute numbers)
- Most LLM usage is "zero-click" (no website visits)
- Focus on "share of answer" not just traffic

---

## **What Doesn't Work**

- **llm.txt files:** No major LLM officially supports this
- **Keyword stuffing / entity stuffing**
- **Hiding text** (old-school SEO tactics)
- **Mass AI-generated pages** without quality control
- **Generic backlinks** - context of mention matters more than link itself

---

## **Specific Testing Recommendations**

1. Use ChatGPT to summarize your articles - if it misses key points, revise your TL;DR
2. Test fanout: Ask LLM a complex question, see what simpler queries it might generate
3. Monitor competitors' mentions in AI outputs using tools like Brand Radar
4. Track which pages get LLM referral traffic vs traditional search
5. Check if FAQ sections answer questions the way users ask LLMs

---

## **Model-Specific Notes**

- **Different LLMs have different preferences:** Only 11% domain citation overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity
- **Start with one platform:** Optimize for ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews first
- **Temperature/randomness:** Same query can yield different brand mentions each time
- **Personalization:** LLMs remember past conversations, affecting brand mentions

---

**Core principle:** LLMs reward specificity, recency, structure, and off-site validation over traditional on-site SEO factors. Think "teaching" rather than "ranking."
