# Mapping what we know against B2B buying process from Negacz

### ✅ Stage 1: Problem/Wyzwanie (STRONG COVERAGE)

**What we know from the JTBD:**

**Primary Triggers:**

- Software becomes a limiter (not an enabler) → Critical pain, clear buying triggerT
- Quality degrades with scale → "When you increase scale, quality unfortunately drops"
- Data scattered across 3-5 systems → Fragmented visibility
- Slow feedback loop for changes → Weeks/months vs hours needed
- Reactive instead of proactive service delivery

**Emotional Triggers:**

- Feel in control despite complexity
- Reduce anxiety about technology investments (400-450K PLN mentioned)
- Trust that growth won't break operations

**Gap:** While we understand the problems well, we're missing **quantified thresholds** - at what point does each pain become severe enough to trigger action?
